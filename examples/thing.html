This surface has never been drawn, which is why it is drawn here.

The studio’s prototype kit covers every screen the product already has, and none of them covers what a customer sees after a class is called off. When something is already drawn, this page links or frames that view instead — drawing a second version of an existing screen is how two versions start disagreeing.

Both screens are real files drawn on the prototype kit, not pictures and not markup improvised for this report. They inherit the same tokens the real components use, they follow light and dark on their own, and either one can be moved into the kit as a new screen without being redrawn.

Should these two screens go into the prototype kit?
They would sit beside every other screen, where the next person looks first — rather than living inside one report.
