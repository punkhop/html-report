Everything but the writing sitting is finished.

Every report page runs on one stylesheet now.

Yesterday every page carried its own styling, written onto each element one at a time, so changing the center changed nothing. Now every page links one stylesheet and inherits every fix made to it. Measured before the rebuild: 1,627 styles written onto elements across nine pages, and not one CSS class anywhere in any of them.

That is the difference the last two days bought. Not new pages — a center that the pages hang off.
